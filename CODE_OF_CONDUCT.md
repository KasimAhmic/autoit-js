# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, are committed to fostering a collaborative, productive, and respectful environment. We recognize that open-source collaboration often involves direct, no-nonsense communication. While discussions may be terse or blunt, they should never be hostile, discriminatory, or personal.

Constructive criticism and disagreements are part of the process—but personal attacks, harassment, or abuse are not tolerated. We aim for an environment where contributors can engage honestly and efficiently without walking on eggshells, yet with basic respect intact.

## Our Standards

Examples of acceptable behavior:

- Providing constructive feedback, even if blunt or direct
- Offering corrections without sugarcoating, while remaining respectful
- Expressing disagreement without personal insults or inflammatory remarks
- Acknowledging mistakes and moving on without unnecessary drama
- Prioritizing project goals over personal feelings

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Personal attacks or name-calling
- Harassment, whether public or private
- Deliberate hostility or aggression toward contributors
- Dismissing others’ contributions without constructive feedback
- Using slurs, discriminatory language, or targeting individuals based on personal characteristics
- Enforcement Responsibilities
- Community leaders are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate corrective action when those standards are breached. While terse communication is acceptable, hostility, sustained negativity, or personal abuse is not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces—GitHub issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and any project-related communications, both public and private.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the project maintainers at kasim.ahmic@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled discreetly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will consider intent, frequency, and severity when determining consequences.

| Impact        | Consequence                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Correction    | A single instance of unnecessarily harsh or unproductive communication. A private reminder to keep feedback focused on code and solutions, not people.   |
| Warning       | Repeated bluntness that crosses into hostility or dismissiveness. A formal warning; further violations may lead to temporary exclusion from discussions. |
| Temporary Ban | Continued hostility or personal attacks despite warnings. Temporary suspension from participation in the project.                                        |
| Permanent Ban | Sustained abusive behavior or severe incidents. Permanent exclusion from all project-related activities.                                                 |

## Final Notes

This code acknowledges that collaboration isn’t always sunshine and rainbows—but civility is non-negotiable. Terseness is fine; disrespect isn’t.
